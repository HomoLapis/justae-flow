# Name and logo

The names "JustAE" and "JustAE Flow" and the JustAE logo (the files `client/public/justae-logo*.svg` and
`client/public/favicon.svg`, and the three bars the EffectCraft panel draws) are not covered by the source code
licence. You may say that your work is based on JustAE Flow, in plain text. A modified version you distribute must
use its own name and logo.
